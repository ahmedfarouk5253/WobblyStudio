# 01: Product Vision

## Name and identity
- **Working title:** Arrow Atlas. Play Store title: **"Arrow Atlas: Arrow Escape Puzzle"** (30 chars max on Play).
- **Studio:** Wobbly Studio. Package `com.wobblystudio.arrowatlas`.
- **Mascot:** **Pip**, a small round explorer bird with a coral scarf and a satchel. Pip's tail is an arrowhead. Arrows that leave the board "fly away like Pip's flock".
- **Fiction (light, never in the way):** Pip is travelling the world collecting postcards. Each chapter is a destination. Clearing levels paints that destination's postcard into your **Atlas**.
- **Before launch:** search Play for the name. If "Arrow Atlas" is taken, the backups are **Arrow Voyage**, **Pip's Arrow Trip** and **Arrowscape Atlas**.

## One-line pitch
> The calm, fair arrow puzzle. Clear the board, paint the world.

## Target players
1. **Primary: relaxers, 25–60, ~60% women.** They play to unwind, often in the evening or while commuting. They value calm, readability, fairness and no pressure, and they hate ads that interrupt.
2. **Secondary: puzzle grinders.** They want harder puzzles, Nightmare boards, streaks and personal bests. They'll pay $4 to remove ads without thinking twice.
3. **Tertiary: players over 55.** They need big touch targets, high contrast and no tiny arrows. They're an underserved, high-value US/EU audience.

Markets: global English first; 12 languages at launch (doc 13). The top ad-revenue markets are US, UK, DE, CA, AU, JP and KR. Volume comes from IN, BR, ID, MX and TR.

## Design pillars
1. **Instantly understood.** No rules to read. The direction is printed on the piece, and level 1 teaches by doing.
2. **Fair, always.** Nothing ever feels like a trick or a cash grab. Mistakes come from the player's thinking, never from the touch screen, an ad or an unfair rule.
3. **Calm with a spark.** No timers by default, soft sounds, a satisfying whoosh, and a small celebration at the right moments.
4. **Always something new.** A new board element every 25 levels, picture-shaped landmark boards, and a postcard to reveal. Hard levels feel different, not just bigger.
5. **Small, fast, offline.** Under 40 MB install. Runs smoothly on a $100 Android phone. Fully playable offline.

## The 12 differentiators (each one answers a real complaint)
| # | Feature | Complaint it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Smart Touch + Precision Loupe.** Taps snap to the right arrow; on small cells a magnifier lets you confirm before firing. | "99% of my lost health is from the wrong arrow getting hit" |
| 2 | **Fit-to-screen + minimap + Fit button** on big boards | "Zoom in, can't see the puzzle; zoom out, can't tap" |
| 3 | **Second Chance.** The first heart lost in each level can be refunded for free. | "You really only have two times to mess up" |
| 4 | **Zen mode.** No hearts at all, for anyone, free. | "Can we get a no-health mode already?" |
| 5 | **Fair Ads Pledge**, shown in-game and on the store page | Ads after every level, banners over the board |
| 6 | **Remove Ads ($3.99) that really removes ads**, plus free daily refills | "Paid for no ads, still had to watch ads" |
| 7 | **8 board elements** (rocks, locks, ice, mirrors, keys & gates, portals, one-way gates) | "Hard just means more arrows crammed in", "repetitive" |
| 8 | **Difficulty choice.** Daily in 3 sizes; Endless in Easy, Medium, Hard and Nightmare. | "Let me play Nightmare all the time", "too easy" |
| 9 | **Dark mode, colored arrows, colorblind palettes, arrow thickness.** Free forever. | Easybrain removing colors and dark mode |
| 10 | **Atlas postcards + landmark boards** shaped like the place | Repetitive, no sense of progress |
| 11 | **Personal-best timer** (optional) and **perfect-clear crowns** | Players asking for a timer and goals |
| 12 | **A promise not to take things away.** Free features stay free; rewards never get nerfed. | "Updated, now only one extra try" |

## What we deliberately copy from the leaders (the proven core)
- Bent multi-cell arrows. The exit check is the straight ray from the head. The body follows snake-style.
- A dot grid behind the board. Minimal look. No timer.
- 3 hearts per level. Hint = highlight one movable arrow.
- Daily puzzle with a streak, a streak freeze and a monthly trophy.
- Very fast early levels: 10–25 s each for the first 15 levels.
- Remove Ads as the main purchase.

## What we deliberately do NOT do
- No energy or lives across levels. No forced competitions or leagues (an *optional* league may come post-launch).
- No banners. No ads in the first 15 levels. No ads after a failed level.
- No coin packs over $19.99, no fail-offer popups, no loot boxes, no countdown offers.
- No copied levels, art, names or store text from other games.
- No real-money or cash-out gimmicks.

## Brand voice
Warm, short and encouraging. Pip speaks rarely, in 3–6 words ("Nice one!", "Try the edges first", "See you tomorrow!"). Never guilt-trip ("Don't give up now!!") and never shout.
