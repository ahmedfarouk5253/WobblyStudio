About the developer

The person behind the wobble.

A

Hi, I'm the developer behind Wobbly Studio. I'm an independent, one-person Android game studio — I design, build, and publish every game myself, from the first sketch to the Play Store listing.

I love making small, polished, low-pressure games: puzzles you can pick up for five minutes or an hour, without ads getting in the way of the fun or the game asking too much of your time. Every game on this site was made by me, independently.
