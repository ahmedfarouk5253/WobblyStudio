Arrow Voyage

Arrow Escape Puzzle

Tap an arrow. If its path is clear, it flies away. Clear the board to win. Arrow Voyage is the calm, fair arrow escape puzzle. Easy to learn, surprisingly deep, and made to be relaxing. Every level you clear paints part of a travel postcard, across 20 hand-painted destinations with Pip, the little explorer bird.

Smart Touch & Fair Design

Taps go to the arrow you meant. On huge boards, a magnifier lets you aim before you fire. The first slip in every level can be undone for free. No hearts, no pressure. Zen Mode is free for everyone.

500 Levels Across 20 Destinations

New board elements every chapter: rocks, locks, ice, mirrors, keys & gates, portals, one-way gates. Landmark levels shaped like the places you visit. Watch your skills grow as you explore hand-painted worlds.

Daily Puzzle & Beyond the Map

Daily Puzzle in 3 sizes, streaks and monthly trophies. Beyond the Map offers endless puzzles from Easy to Nightmare difficulty for those who want more.

Fill Your Travel Journal

Every level you clear paints part of a travel postcard. Collect all 20 stamps with Pip, the little explorer bird, across the hand-painted destinations.

Accessibility & Comfort

No timers. Play offline, anywhere. Dark & light themes, colorblind-safe colors, and thicker arrows — all free, forever.

Our Ad Promise

No ads in your first 15 levels. Never an ad in the middle of a puzzle, and no banners over the board. Never an ad right after you fail. Videos are always optional — and always give the reward. Remove Ads removes every forced ad, forever.
