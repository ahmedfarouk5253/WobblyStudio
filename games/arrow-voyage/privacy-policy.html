Privacy Policy — Arrow Voyage

Last updated: October 4, 2026

In short:

  • We don't collect, store, or share any personal information.
  • There's no account, no sign-in, and no server — your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The only device permission used is Vibration, for haptic feedback.
  • If we ever add ads, purchases, or analytics, this page will be updated first.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Arrow Voyage (package name: com.arrowvoyage), developed by Wobbly Studio ("we", "us"). It explains what information the app collects, uses, and stores — and what it does not.

2. Information we do not collect

Arrow Voyage does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect, and the app does not transmit anywhere:

  • Your name, email address, or contact details
  • Your location
  • Photos, contacts, or files on your device
  • Any personal identifiers

The app has no backend server, so there is nothing for this information to be sent to even if it existed.

3. Information stored on your device

Your game progress — solved puzzles, unlocked travel locations, and settings — is saved in a local file on your own device only. This data never leaves your device, is not visible to us or to anyone else, and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app.

Because everything is stored locally, there is no account or server-side data for us to access, transfer, or delete on your behalf — uninstalling the app removes it completely.

4. Permissions the app uses

PermissionWhat it's used for
VibrationUsed only for haptic feedback during gameplay (e.g. solving a puzzle or unlocking a location). No data is read or transmitted through this permission, and it can be turned off in the game's Settings.

The app requests no other permissions — no camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage access beyond its own save file.

5. Third-party services

As of this version, Arrow Voyage does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Because the app is distributed through Google Play, Google's own services (such as Google Play Store, Play Protect, and basic crash/diagnostic reporting built into Android) may collect standard technical data under Google's own privacy policy, independent of anything in the app itself. This is the same for every Android app and is outside of our control. You can review how Google handles this data in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. If we add ads, purchases, or analytics later

Future updates may introduce optional in-app purchases (handled entirely through Google Play's own billing system, which processes payment details directly — we never see your card information), rewarded video ads, or basic anonymous analytics to understand how the game is played. If any of these are added:

  • This policy will be updated before that update is released
  • The specific third-party providers used will be named here, along with what data they collect
  • The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly

We recommend checking this page after any major update if you have concerns.

7. Children's privacy

Arrow Voyage is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families program. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, and — as described above — we do not collect personal information from any user regardless of age.

8. Data protection regulations (GDPR, CCPA, and similar)

Because we do not collect personal information, most obligations under regulations such as the EU's GDPR or California's CCPA are not triggered by this app in its current form. This statement describes the app's current behavior and is not a legal certification of compliance. If you have specific concerns about your rights under a particular law, or if you are the developer adding features that do collect data, consult a legal professional.

9. Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data at risk of being exposed by this app. Your device's own security (screen lock, OS updates) protects your local save file the same way it protects any other app data on your phone.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when new features are added. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the app's data practices, contact us at wobblygames45@gmail.com.
