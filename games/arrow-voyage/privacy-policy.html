Privacy Policy — Arrow Voyage

Last updated: October 4, 2026

In short:

  • There is no account system, and we never ask for your name or email address.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Firebase to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes.
  • Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Your progress is saved on your device, and in your Google account only if you use Play Games.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Arrow Voyage (package name: com.arrowvoyage), made by Wobbly Studio, a one-person indie studio ("we", "us"). It explains what data the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any content you create. The game has no chat and no user-generated content.

3. Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings and coins are saved in a file on your device. If you use Play Games, a copy of your save is kept in your Google account so you can restore it on another device.

4. Ads and consent

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. If you don't consent, you may still see non-personalized ads. Buying "Remove Ads" turns off all interstitial ads.

Firebase Analytics is told not to use the advertising ID unless you have given consent.

7. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months, and crash reports for up to 90 days. Your Play Games save stays in your Google account until you delete it there.

8. Your choices and rights (including data deletion)

  • Change or withdraw ad consent in Settings → Privacy options (shown where consent applies).
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • Delete your local data by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage.
  • Delete your Play Games data from your Google account settings.
  • To ask about, access or delete analytics data linked to your device, email ahmedfarouk5253@gmail.com with the subject "Arrow Voyage data deletion". We reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have further rights, and we will honor them.

We do not sell your personal information.

9. Children's privacy

Arrow Voyage is made for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. It is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data sent to Google's services is encrypted in transit. Your device's own security (screen lock, OS updates) protects the save file on your phone.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date at the top.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the game's data practices, contact us at ahmedfarouk5253@gmail.com.
