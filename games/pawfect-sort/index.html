Pawfect Sort

Cozy Pet Sorting Puzzle

Carry adorable animals between cushions. Get three of the same kind together and they cuddle up and drift away in a puff of hearts. Clear the board to win — then do it again, forever. Pawfect Sort is a cozy triple-sort puzzle with no timers you can fail, no lives to run out, and no wall you have to pay to get past.

Endless Levels, Every One Solvable

Levels are built fresh on your phone, so the game never runs out. And every single one is provably beatable — we verified twenty thousand of them in a row. If you ever truly wedge yourself in, the game notices and hands you a free rewind.

It Starts Gentle and Grows With You

Level one is one little family on three cushions. From there it slowly opens up — more animals, tighter space, animals hidden in burrows you have to dig out. By the time it gets genuinely clever, you'll be ready for it.

90 Cute Animals to Rehome

Cats, pandas, foxes, red pandas, capybaras, axolotls, narwhals… a new friend unlocks every few levels, all the way up. Fill your Friends album by playing.

Build Your Sanctuary

Every level you clear well earns stars, and stars build a little animal sanctuary — a meadow, a cottage, a pond, a treetop, six areas in all. Somewhere for all those rehomed friends to live.

A New Board Every Day

Today's Challenge is the same puzzle for everyone, seeded from the date. Keep a daily streak going for bigger rewards.

Kind By Design

No fail state. The optional clock only decides a bonus star, and you can switch it off. Rewind is free and unlimited. A booster that wouldn't actually help is refunded, not spent. Every animal is told apart by both colour and shape, so the board stays readable.
