Sparkfold

2048 Merge Quest

Pip, the last ember sprite, is travelling through five worlds to rekindle the Great Hearth that once lit them all. Swipe to merge tiles in a roguelike twist on 2048: every board has a goal and a move limit, and every run is different.

A Roguelike Run in Every World

Each world is a short map of 2048-style boards with goals and move limits. Choose your path, face an Elite and beat the Boss to rescue the world's Keeper.

Draft Charms That Bend the Rules

Between boards, pick Charms, shop, rest at the campfire or open a treasure. Combine Charms to build a run that is entirely your own.

Five Worlds, Five Twists

Every world brings its own look, music and one twist mechanic. The tiles stay easy to read everywhere, with a colorblind mode available.

Rebuild the Hearth

Rescued Keepers move into the Hearth, your cozy home village. Restore buildings, read letters and dress Pip in new outfits.

Daily Expedition

Everyone gets the same seed once a day. Keep your streak going and share your result card with friends.

Fair and Friendly

Free to play, with optional ads and in-app purchases. Purchases never affect the ranked Daily Expedition, and there are no loot boxes. Available in English and Arabic.
