Putt Worlds

Mini Golf

Grab your putter and join Gus the gopher on a mini golf adventure across 10 colourful worlds. Putt World is a relaxing, top-down mini golf game you play with one finger. Drag back to aim, let go to putt, and sink the ball in as few strokes as you can.

150 Hand-Crafted Holes

Explore 10 themed worlds, from Green Meadow to space. Every hole is designed to be relaxing yet challenging, with short levels perfect for quick breaks.

Tricky Obstacles

Navigate windmills, ramps, moving walls, ice, sand, water and more. Each obstacle type brings new strategic challenges to master.

Earn Stars & Go for Holes-in-One

Earn up to 3 stars on every hole by finishing in fewer strokes. Chase the perfect holes-in-one for the ultimate mini golf achievement.

Helpful Powerups

Use Aim Guide, Mulligan and Magnet powerups to help with the hardest holes. Every hole can be finished without powerups or ads.

Collect & Customize

Collect fun golf balls and trails to personalize your game. Earn daily rewards, coins and achievements as you play.

Play Offline, Anytime

No internet connection needed. Play whenever you want, wherever you want. Optional ads and in-app purchases are available for those who want them.
