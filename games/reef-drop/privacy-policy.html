Privacy Policy — Reef Drop

Last updated: October 4, 2026

In short:

  • Reef Drop has no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email, or location.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob), offers optional in-app purchases (Google Play Billing), and uses Google Firebase for anonymous analytics and crash reports.
  • Those services use your device's advertising ID and technical information, as described below. You can choose your ad privacy options in the game's Settings.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Reef Drop (package name: com.reefdrop), developed by Wobbly Studio ("we", "us"). It explains what information the app and the third-party services it uses collect, why, and what choices you have.

2. Information we do not collect

Reef Drop does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not ask for or collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contact details
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or files on your device

We do not run our own server that stores personal data about players.

3. Information stored on your device

Your game progress — reef, creatures discovered, scores, coins, settings, and a randomly generated anonymous player ID — is saved in local storage on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

4. Information collected by third-party services

The app includes the following third-party services. Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

ServicePurposeData
Google AdMob Showing rewarded, interstitial, and banner ads; ad measurement; consent Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, approximate location derived from IP. If you consent (where required), ads may be personalized.
Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) Understanding how the game is played, fixing crashes, adjusting game settings remotely App interactions and gameplay events (for example, level completed), crash logs and diagnostics, device model and OS version, app instance ID.
Google Play Billing Processing and restoring in-app purchases Purchase history (products bought) is handled by Google Play. Payment details are handled only by Google Play; we never see your card information.

This data is used to operate the game, show ads, process purchases, measure and improve the game, and keep it stable. We do not sell your personal information.

Data is encrypted in transit. Because we cannot identify you from the information the game holds, we cannot look up or delete individual records on request; you can reset the advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads) and delete local data by uninstalling the app.

5. Ads and your choices

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland), the game shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time using Privacy options in the game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android device settings.

You can remove interstitial and banner ads permanently with the optional "Remove Ads" purchase.

6. Permissions the app uses

PermissionWhat it's used for
Internet / network stateLoading ads, analytics, crash reports, and purchases.
Advertising IDUsed by Google AdMob for ads and ad measurement. You can reset or delete it in your Android settings.
VibrationHaptic feedback during gameplay; can be turned off in the game's Settings.
NotificationsOptional reminders (for example, lives refilled or a daily reward). The game asks first, and you can turn them off in Android settings.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage access. When you choose to share a screenshot of your progress, the image is passed to Android's share sheet and goes only to the app you pick.

7. Children's privacy

Reef Drop is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help get it removed from our records.

8. Your rights (GDPR, CCPA, and similar)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of personal data, and to withdraw consent. You can withdraw ad consent in the game's Settings (Privacy options) and control the advertising ID in your Android settings. For other requests, contact us using the address below. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond the ad services described above.

9. Security

Data sent by the app to the services above is encrypted in transit. Your local save file is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, OS updates). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when new features are added. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the app's data practices, contact us at wobblygames45@gmail.com.
