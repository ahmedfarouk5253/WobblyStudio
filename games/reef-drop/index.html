Reef Drop

Ocean Merge Game

Dive into Reef Drop, a relaxing drop-and-merge puzzle set deep in the ocean. Drop sea creatures into the tank and merge two matching ones to evolve them into something bigger and rarer. Chain merges, clear each level's goal and watch your reef come back to life.

How to Play

Aim and drop creatures into the tank. Two matching creatures merge into the next one in the chain. Reach the level goal before the tank overflows. Chain merges for bigger scores and bonus rewards.

160 Handcrafted Levels

New goals, ice, clams and tricky blockers in each level. Every puzzle is designed to challenge you in different ways as you progress deeper into the ocean.

Boss Levels

Face special challenges with unique mechanics. Test your skills against powerful opponents to advance further.

Collect Every Creature

Discover and collect every sea creature in your album. Evolve creatures through merging to unlock the full ecosystem of the deep.

Rebuild Your Reef

Earn stars to rebuild and decorate each reef area. Turn each level's completion into a visual reward as your reef comes back to life.

Easy to Pick Up, Tricky to Master

One-thumb play in portrait mode. Glossy, colorful ocean art and calm, relaxing sounds create the perfect escape. Play for a quick break or a long dive.
