Privacy Policy — Bank Shot: Ricochet Puzzle

Effective November 27, 2026

Bank Shot: Ricochet Puzzle ("the game") is made by Wobbly Studio ("we"). This policy explains what information is processed when you play, in plain language.

The short version

No account needed. We don’t collect your name, email, or any personal details. Your progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is saved only on your device. The game uses a few Google services for ads, anonymous statistics, crash reports and purchases, described below. We run no servers of our own.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location from the IP address, and ad interactions to show and measure ads and to detect fraud. Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for it through Google’s consent form after your first level, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. Without consent, ads are not personalised. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Analytics and crash reports

The game uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand how the game is used and to fix crashes. This includes anonymous data such as app usage events (for example, a level completed), a randomly generated app-instance identifier, device type, operating system version and crash logs. It does not include your name, email or contact details. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google, not by us or the game. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed, so we can unlock the matching content. See the Google Privacy Policy.

What’s stored on your device

The game keeps your progress, coins, stars, purchases and settings in local storage on your device. If you uninstall the game, this data is deleted.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The game requests Internet and network state access only to load ads, process purchases and send analytics and crash reports. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data ourselves. Analytics data is kept by Firebase for the retention period set in our Firebase project. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings. To ask about deletion of analytics data, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us at wobblygames45@gmail.com.
