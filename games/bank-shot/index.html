Bank Shot

Ricochet Puzzle

Aim. Bounce. Ricochet. Bank Shot is a one-thumb ricochet puzzle: drag to aim, let go, and watch your ball bounce off walls, bumpers and blocks to collect every gem on the board. Every level has a clever shot waiting to be found, and every shot plays out exactly the same each time you fire it.

120 Hand-Made Levels in 6 Worlds

Toy Box, Jelly Lagoon, Crystal Cave, Clockwork Carnival, Neon Arcade and Star Station. Each world adds new tricks: jelly blocks, portals and mirror walls, spinning bars and sliding blocks, numbered gems, one-way gates, bombs and splitters. Every world has 20 levels, and the last one is a boss.

No Timer, No Pressure

Take your time. The ball always flies the same way for the same aim, so planning pays off. Think it through, then make the ball do something beautiful.

Earn Three Stars

Clear a level in par shots for three stars. Stars open new worlds.

Helpful Powerups, Never Required

Hint shows the winning aim, Rewind takes back a shot, Trace reveals the whole path and Power Ball smashes straight through blocks. Every level can be solved without them.

Play Offline

No internet is needed to play. Collect ball skins and trails, claim a daily reward and come back for new worlds.
