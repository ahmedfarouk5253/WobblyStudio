Zoozle: Ai Jigsaw Puzzles

Effective August 24, 2026 · Published by Wobbly Studio

Zoozle is a jigsaw puzzle game for players of all ages, including children. This policy explains what information the app touches, why, and how it's kept to a minimum — in plain language, not legal boilerplate.

01 The short version

No account or sign-in No personal data collected by us Progress stays on your device

Zoozle doesn't ask for your name, email, or any personal details. Your puzzle progress, stars, streaks, and unlocked characters are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to us. The only data that leaves your device goes to Google's ad and payment services described below.

02 Advertising

Zoozle shows ads through Google AdMob. Because the app doesn't verify a player's age, every ad request is sent tagged as child-directed: AdMob is instructed not to use behavioral or interest-based advertising, not to serve remarketing ads, and to cap content at a general (G) rating for every player, regardless of age. This follows Google Play's Families Policy.

AdMob may still collect technical identifiers (such as an advertising ID or IP-based general location) to serve and measure ads, and to detect fraud. Google's own privacy practices for this are described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

03 Purchases

Zoozle's in-app purchases (Remove Ads, Zoozle Bundle, Power Up Pack) are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google, not by us or our app — we only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed, so we can unlock the matching content.

04 What's stored on your device

Zoozle keeps everything about your play session — level progress, stars, streaks, unlocked characters, power-up inventory, and settings — in local storage on your device only. Uninstalling the app removes this data. We have no server that stores or receives it.

05 Children's privacy

Zoozle is designed to be appropriate for players under 13, and is built to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising shown to all players is treated as child-directed, as described in section 2.

06 Permissions

Zoozle requests Internet and network state access solely to load ads, process purchases, and (optionally) submit an in-game rating through Google Play's native review prompt. No other device permissions are requested.

07 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new effective date above. Continued use of Zoozle after a change means you accept the revised policy.

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us at wobblygames45@gmail.com
