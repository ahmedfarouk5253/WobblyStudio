Zoozle

AI Jigsaw Puzzle

Zoozle is a cozy jigsaw puzzle game starring an ever-growing cast of AI-generated animal characters — each one a cute, funny critter with its own silly profession and a one-line bio. Piece together a fox chef, an astronaut panda, and dozens more as you relax and unwind.

120 Hand-Built Levels

Work through hand-built levels laid out in a two-column grid, with piece counts ramping from a gentle 5x5 up to a challenging 10x10. Complete levels to earn stars and unlock new characters into your collection.

Daily Puzzles

A brand-new puzzle every day, seeded to today's date. Pick your own difficulty — 36, 81, 144, or 225 pieces — and replay any difficulty as often as you like. Build a daily streak, chase your best streak, and browse a full monthly calendar of past puzzles.

My Collection

Every level and daily puzzle you complete unlocks its character into your personal gallery. Flip through your growing collection of animal characters and read their bios.

Achievements

28 achievements across 8 tracks — puzzles completed, pieces placed, play time, perfect levels, daily streaks, characters unlocked, power-ups used, and total stars — with a satisfying in-game unlock toast every time you hit a milestone.

Power-Ups

Stuck on a tricky puzzle? Use Hints, the Edge Finder, extra time, or Vision to see the full picture. Out of power-ups? Watch a quick video to top up for free.
