Privacy Policy — Neon Divide

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Wobbly Studio ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the Android game Neon Divide (the "App"). Wobbly Studio is an independent, one-person game studio. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. Depending on the features you use, the App and its third-party services may collect:

  • Device information — device model, operating system version, language, and general region, collected automatically for app performance and compatibility.
  • Advertising identifiers — such as the Google Advertising ID, used by our advertising and analytics partners to serve and measure ads, where applicable.
  • Usage and diagnostic data — such as crash reports, gameplay statistics, and performance data, used to improve stability and the overall experience.
  • Gameplay progress — such as high scores and level completion, which may be stored locally on your device or, if you sign in with Google Play Games, synced to your account so your progress carries across devices.

We do not require you to create an account to play, and we do not knowingly collect names, email addresses, or other directly identifying information unless you voluntarily provide them (for example, by emailing us for support).

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you, in accordance with their own privacy policies. These may include:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Play Services / Google Play Games Services
  • Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics (analytics and crash reporting)

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these providers to understand how they handle your information.

How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App
  • To display and personalize advertising, where applicable
  • To understand how the App is used and diagnose technical issues
  • To respond to support requests

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with the third-party service providers listed above solely to provide the functionality described in this policy (such as showing ads or measuring app performance).

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Data Retention & Security

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, and we take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

You can reset or limit your device's advertising identifier through your device's system settings. You can also uninstall the App at any time to stop all data collection by the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
