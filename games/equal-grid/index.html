Equal Grid

Daily Block Puzzle

Same grid. Same blocks. Best score wins. Equal Grid is a calm 8x8 block puzzle built on one simple promise: everybody gets the same challenge. Drag pieces, clear lines, and compete fairly with no boosters, no ads, and no timers.

Today's Grid

Every player gets the identical board and the identical run of pieces, every single day. Three attempts. No timer. Your best score counts. The puzzle is built from the date itself, so it works anywhere — no account, no connection, no waiting for a server.

Zen Grid

No pressure, no clock, no end. Place pieces, clear lines, and stop whenever you like. Your run saves automatically, so you can put your phone down mid-game and pick it up tomorrow.

Simple to Play, Hard to Master

Drag a piece onto the 8x8 board. Fill a complete row or column and it clears instantly. Clear several at once to build combos and push your score higher. There is nothing else to learn — and plenty to get better at.

Fair By Design

No boosters. No revives. No piece rerolls. No paid or ad-based advantage. No extra attempts for money. Everyone plays the same puzzle under the same rules. That is the whole point.

Built to Respect You

Plays completely offline. No account required. No ads. Collects no data at all. Colorblind friendly palette. Reduced-motion mode. Independent sound and vibration controls.
