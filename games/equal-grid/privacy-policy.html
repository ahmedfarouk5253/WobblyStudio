Privacy Policy — Equal Grid

Effective 9 September 2026

Equal Grid is a block puzzle game. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short answer is that it does not take any — and the rest of this page explains exactly what that means, in plain language.

The short version

Equal Grid collects nothing. No account, no sign-in, no name, no email, no advertising identifier, no location. This version of the game does not connect to the internet at all. Your scores, settings and saved run stay in the app's own storage on your phone, and are deleted when you uninstall it.

  • Personal data collected: none
  • Data shared with third parties: none
  • Account required: no
  • Advertising: no ads
  • In-app purchases: none
  • Analytics sent off your device: none

What stays on your device

Equal Grid saves a small amount of information so the game works the way you would expect:

  • Your current Zen run, so you can close the app mid-game and come back to it
  • Your scores and personal bests
  • Daily Grid attempt history
  • Your settings — sound, vibration, reduced motion, and colour palette

All of it is written to the app's private storage area on your own phone. None of it is transmitted anywhere, and we have no server that receives it. Uninstalling Equal Grid removes it permanently.

The daily puzzle works without a server

Every player gets the same Daily Grid because the puzzle is calculated from the date itself, using the same rules on every device — not because your phone downloaded it from us. That is why the daily challenge works with no internet connection and no account.

Permissions

Equal Grid requests two Android permissions:

  • Vibration — used only for the optional haptic feedback when you place a piece or clear a line. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Internet — declared in the app for a planned future online leaderboard. This version of the game never opens a network connection. If a future update begins sending data, this policy and the app's Google Play Data Safety information will be updated before that release ships.

Children

Equal Grid is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your choices

Because nothing leaves your device, there is no account to close and no data for us to hand over or erase on request. Deleting the app deletes everything it ever stored. If you would like to ask about any of this, the contact address is below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date at the top. Any change that affects what the app collects will be made before, not after, the update that introduces it.
