Privacy Policy — Twisty Putt

Last updated: November 26, 2026

In short:

  • There is no account system, and we never ask for your name or email address.
  • Your progress, settings and your answer to the age question are saved on your device only.
  • The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Players who say they are under 13 only get non-personalized, child-directed ads.
  • The game uses no analytics, no in-app purchases and no sign-in.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the game Twisty Putt (Android package name: com.twistyputt, and its web version), made by Wobbly Studio, a one-person indie studio ("we", "us"). It explains what data the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

2. What the game collects and why

DataWhyShared with
Advertising ID, approximate location (from IP address), device information, ad interactionsShowing and measuring ads and preventing ad fraud (Google AdMob). Personalized ads only with your consent where the law requires it, and never for players under 13.Google

That is the only data that leaves your device, and it goes to Google, not to us. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any content you create. The game has no chat and no user-generated content.

3. Information stored on your device

  • Game progress and settings: holes played, stars, coins, sound, vibration and motion settings, and ad-timing counters.
  • Your answer to the age question: only the birth year. It is used on your device to choose child-safe ad settings and is never sent to us.

This is kept in the app's local storage. It is not uploaded to our servers (we have none).

4. Ads and consent

The Android app shows full-screen ads between some holes, plus optional "watch an ad" rewards (undo a shot, extra shots, a hint or skipping a hole). These ads are served by Google AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time with Pause → Privacy choices. If you don't consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

The web version currently shows no third-party ads. If a games website you play it on shows its own ads, that website's privacy policy applies.

5. Children

Twisty Putt is a general-audience game. The first time the app starts, it asks for a birth year without suggesting any answer. If the answer means the player is under 13, every ad request is marked as child-directed: only non-personalized ads with general-audience content are shown, and the advertising ID is not used for personalization. Players under the local age of digital consent (up to 16 in some countries) are flagged to Google in the same way. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help delete it.

7. Third-party services

Google processes ad data as described in its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

8. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

9. Your choices and rights (including data deletion)

  • Change or withdraw ad consent with Pause → Privacy choices (shown where consent applies).
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Delete all game data by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage.
  • Questions or requests: email wobblygames45@gmail.com with the subject "Twisty Putt privacy". We reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have further rights, and we will honor them.

We do not sell your personal information.

10. Security

Data sent to Google's services is encrypted in transit. Your device's own security (screen lock, OS updates) protects the data saved on your phone.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if analytics are added, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date at the top before the change reaches the game.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the game's data practices, contact us at wobblygames45@gmail.com.
