Twisty Putt

Mini Golf Puzzle

Mini golf where every hole has a twist. Drag back, aim and let go, then watch your ball bank off paper walls, warp through portals, ride the wind and slingshot around planets. Each hole takes about 20 seconds and a little bit of genius.

96 Holes, 8 Worlds

From Paper Meadow to Magnet Manor, every world brings a new mechanic: bumpers and spinners, ice walls that crack, conveyor belts and gears, portals, wind and launch pads, gravity wells, and magnets you can flip.

A Puzzle in Every Putt

Every hole is short, but each one hides a clever line. Find it and you can ace it. The last holes remix everything you have learned.

Earn Stars & Go for Aces

Finish under the target for 3 stars, collect stars to open new worlds, and chase holes-in-one on every course.

Handmade Paper Craft

Cut-paper courses, folded windmills, origami fans and paper planets, with soft, tactile sounds. Light, small and quick to load.

Play Offline, Anytime

No internet connection needed to play. Free, with optional ads: watch one only if you want an undo, extra shots or a hint.
